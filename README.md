Official repository for the study titled, "Comprehensive Evaluation of Quantitative Measurements From Automated Deep Segmentations of PSMA PET/CT Images". This work evaluates the clinical impact of our proposed loss function L1DFL beyong the traditional dice overlap metric. 
Please do well to cite our paper and that of Shadab Ahamed (citation below) if you use any portion of this code: Dzikunu, O. K., Toosi, A., Ahamed, S., Harsini, S., Bénard, F., Li, X., & Rahmim, A. (2025). Comprehensive Evaluation of Quantitative Measurements from Automated Deep Segmentations of PSMA PET/CT Images. IEEE Transactions on Radiation and Plasma Medical Sciences.

Many thanks to Shadab Ahamed whose code served as the bedrock on which this pipeline was built: Ahamed, S., Xu, Y., Kurkowska, S., Gowdy, C., Bloise, I., Wilson, D., ... & Rahmim, A. (2023). Comprehensive framework for evaluation of deep neural networks in detection and quantification of lymphoma from PET/CT images: clinical insights, pitfalls, and observer agreement analyses. arXiv preprint arXiv:2311.09614.
